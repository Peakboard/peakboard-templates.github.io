description:
  - This interactive template allows you to apply the 5 Why method digitally within your production environment. The 5 Why analysis is an established Lean Management method used to systematically investigate problems and identify their underlying root causes. The analysis starts with a specific problem or deviation. By repeatedly asking “Why?”, the analysis goes deeper step by step until the root cause of the problem can be identified.

  - Each answer is captured directly within the application, creating a clear and traceable cause-and-effect chain. This allows teams to collaboratively analyse issues such as production downtime, quality deviations, material shortages, or recurring process problems. Completed analyses remain documented and can be reviewed at any time, ensuring that valuable insights are retained and recurring root causes can be identified more easily.

  - Download the template for free and use the 5 Why method for simple, transparent, and structured root cause analysis directly within your production environment.
