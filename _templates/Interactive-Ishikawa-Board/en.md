description:
  - With the interactive Ishikawa Board, you can analyse disruptions, quality deviations, process issues and recurring errors in a systematic and structured way. The Ishikawa diagram is an established Lean Management method used to make potential causes of a problem visible, evaluate them together as a team and derive the necessary corrective actions.

  - Select an existing analysis or create a new problem and assign potential causes to the six classic categories – Manpower, Machine, Material, Method, Measurement and Environment. The causes are visualised directly in the Ishikawa diagram and can be added step by step. This turns an initially broad problem into a clear cause analysis, helping your team identify relationships more quickly and discuss potential root causes in a focused way.

  - Download the template for free and use the Ishikawa method for structured root cause analysis in your day-to-day shopfloor operations.
